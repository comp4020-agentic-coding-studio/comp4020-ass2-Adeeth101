# The month the tank runs dry

**SLOP4761** · Week 9

Water security and storage

---

## You have two curves

**Supply**, from week 7. Twelve rows, in kilolitres.

**Demand**, from week 2. Twelve rows, in kilolitres.

Storage is what turns one into the other.

---

## The balance

**V(t) = min( capacity, V(t−1) + inflow − demand )**

Three-year spin-up. Report year three.

Otherwise you are reporting the volume you guessed.

---

## Three rules

1. **Domestic first.** A shortfall is carted in, and carted water is an import.
2. **Garden gets greywater, then the tank.** A garden shortfall lowers *garden water satisfaction*, not water closure.
3. **Full tank overflows.** Overflow is reported. You cannot spend it.

## Throws away 245. Buys 48.

In the same year.

With a surplus, on paper, of 192 kL.

**The annual balance is not imprecise here. It is the wrong question.**

---

## So build a bigger tank?

Test it. Step the spend and plot the curve.

$4,000 → $9,000 buys 38 more kilolitres.

Water closure moves by **0.06**.

---

## And that is Darwin, the best case

At Canberra, Alice Springs and Adelaide the curve is **flat**.

$2,000 or $10,000 — the same water closure.

Those sites are **supply-limited**, not storage-limited.

---

## A tank cannot hold water that never fell

Canberra's roof: 142 kL.

Its household alone: more than that.

Every tank empties, every year, at any size.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

A flat curve is information.

It says the money belongs somewhere else.

---

## This course got it wrong

The reference designs used to spend $9,000 on storage at every site.

Costed against the full schedule: **$3,704 to $9,545 over budget.**

Past the knee at four sites out of five.

---

## Find your own knee

Step the tank in 10 kL increments.

Plot the indicator against capacity.

Spend where it is still rising. Stop where it is not.

---

## Then break it

The average year contains no droughts.

A design tuned to it is tuned to a year that never happens.

---

## The dry-year dataset

**Synthetic.** Scaled from your site's Bureau statistics toward a low decile.

Not a historical drought. Never describe it as one.

Its job is to make your design fail somewhere visible.

---

## What leaves this studio

A sized storage system.

The month it empties, named.

Volume imported. Volume overflowed.

**The same figures re-run on the dry year.**
