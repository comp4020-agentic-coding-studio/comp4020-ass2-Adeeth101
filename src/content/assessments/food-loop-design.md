## The brief

> Design the food half of your preset site's loop — garden, mushrooms, insects,
> fish and preservation — and report how much of the household's food energy and
> protein it closes, net of everything you had to bring in.

Weeks 1 to 6, assembled. The water and waste halves come later; here the water
weeks are still ahead of you, so irrigation supply is a stated assumption rather
than a sized system, and you say so.

The judgement being tested is **whether your subsystems are sized against each
other rather than each against an ideal**. A grow bed sized for its plants and a
fish tank sized for its fish will not agree about feed, and the place they
disagree is where the marks are.

## What you submit

A single document, plus your spreadsheet.

1. **Parameter sheet.** Your site's plot, growing area, roof, existing inventory
   and twelve rows of monthly climate, with the Bureau of Meteorology station
   number and a source for every value.
2. **Five sized subsystems** — perennials and annual garden, mycology, insects,
   aquaponics, preservation — each with its assumptions stated separately from its
   results.
3. **A mass balance** in dry matter, with every stream given a destination and any
   loss term named as a process rather than left as a residual.
4. **Food energy and protein closure**, computed net of imports, against 3.49
   million kcal and 67.5 kg of protein a year.
5. **Nutrient flows** into the garden from compost, frass and spent substrate, and
   what the garden cannot absorb.
6. **A partial energy-load list**: counted loads with power and duty cycle,
   uncertain loads with an estimated range, and omitted loads named. Partial is
   expected at week 7. Silent is not.
7. **A costing** from the [published price schedule](/method/costing/), with the
   10 % contingency.
8. **A ±20 % yield sensitivity**: what your closure figures do if the reference
   yield is wrong in either direction, and which conclusions survive.
9. **One troubleshooting dataset** with a diagnosis. The course supplies a
   [synthetic aquaponic cycling log](/method/datasets/); read it, say what went
   wrong and in what order, and say what you would change.

Indicative length: 2,500 to 3,500 words excluding tables, drawings and the
spreadsheet.

## How performance bands are used

Your reported food energy and protein closure are placed in your site's
[reference bands](/method/). **A band is evidence inside the criteria above, not a
score.** It contributes to *Calculations* and *Loop integration* by showing whether
your numbers are plausible for your site — nothing converts a band into a mark.

A band 2 design with honest assumptions and a closed balance marks above a band 3
design whose numbers cannot be reproduced. That is not encouragement; it is how
the criteria weights work.

## What loses marks

- Reporting gross fish protein rather than the share grown on on-site feed.
- A mass balance that closes because a residual line absorbs the error.
- Assuming irrigation water that weeks 7 to 9 have not yet shown you have.
- Presenting a course design rule as a legal requirement, or the reverse.
- An energy list with no uncertain loads on it. There are always uncertain loads.
