<QuizQuestion

  options={["Lower bioconversion efficiency in exchange for continuity", "A climate advantage is converted into a counted energy load", "It requires imported substrate rather than household scraps", "It voids the course's substrate rule"]}

  wrong="Bioconversion efficiency is a property of the substrate and the larvae, not of where the bin sits."

<QuizQuestion
  n={3}
  question="Which statement about Australian restricted-animal-material and swill rules is correct as they apply to a household aquaponic system?"
  options={["They prohibit feeding insects reared on catering waste to any animal", "They bind pigs and ruminants, and say nothing about fish, aquaculture or insects", "They require insects used as feed to be heat-treated", "They apply only to commercial operations, not to households"]}
  correct={1}
  why="The prohibitions are specific: vertebrate-derived material must not be fed to ruminants, and mammal products must not be fed to pigs. Swill feeding is prohibited nationally and expressly extends to pet and hobby animals. None of it reaches fish, aquaculture or insects. The course's plant-based-substrate and heat-treatment rules are therefore *course design rules*, stricter than the law, adopted for a biosecurity reason."
  wrong="'Commercial only' is wrong in the opposite direction: swill rules explicitly cover pet pigs and backyard poultry. The limit is which animal, not which scale."
/>

<QuizQuestion
  n={4}
  question="What does the FAO feed-rate ratio actually size?"
  options={["The fish tank volume", "The grow-bed area, from the daily feed input", "The number of fish, from the tank volume", "The daily water top-up"]}
  correct={1}
  why="The ratio is grams of feed per square metre of grow bed per day — 40 to 50 for leafy greens and 50 to 80 for fruiting vegetables. Feed drives the nutrient supply, and the bed is sized to consume it. Tank volume is constrained separately by the 20 kg per 1,000 L stocking limit."
  wrong="Sizing from the tank is the intuitive direction and the wrong one. The plants are the consumer of what the feed becomes."
/>

## The mechanics

Sat in the week 5 tutorial, at the start of the session, on paper. Twenty
minutes, 5 multiple-choice questions, no calculators and no numeric working —
if a question can be answered by arithmetic, it is not on this quiz.

Worth **8 %**. Five quizzes at 8 % make up 40 % of the course, spread across weeks
3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 so that keeping up is worth more than catching up.

**This website does not collect or store any answers.** It is a static site with
no server behind it. The practice questions above reveal their own answers in the
page, for your use only; nothing you do here is recorded, marked or transmitted
anywhere, and the graded quiz happens on paper in the room.
