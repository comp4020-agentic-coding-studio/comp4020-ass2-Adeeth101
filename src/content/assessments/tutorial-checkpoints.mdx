## What this is

Each tutorial from week 2 to week 11 produces one small, named output. Submit it
and you have the checkpoint. Ten of them, 2 % each, **20 % of the course**.

This is one assessment record with ten parts, not ten separate assessments. It
exists because both assignments are assembled from these outputs, and a student
who builds them week by week writes the assignment; a student who does not,
writes it from nothing in the last fortnight.

Each is due **5 pm on the Friday of its own teaching week**. The

check your own before you submit.

## What counts as complete

Completion is judged on good faith, not on being right:

- **Every listed output is attempted and legible.** A table with rows in it, a
  curve with axes, a balance with both sides written down.
- **Assumptions are stated.** Where you guessed, say you guessed and say what
  you guessed.
- **Units are on every quantity.** A number without a unit is not an attempt.
- **Working is visible.** Not polished, not typeset — visible.

An honest attempt that turns out to be wrong is **complete**. You get written
feedback on what is wrong, and you carry the corrected version forward. That is
the whole design: errors found in week 4 cost you nothing, and errors found in
the assignment cost you marks.

**Attendance alone is not completion**, and completion does not require
attendance. If you miss a tutorial, do the exercise from the tutorial page —
every one lists what to open, what to do and what the output is, and every one
carries a small labelled practice example so a missing earlier output does not
block you — and submit it by the Friday. The
[extension and late policy](/policies/) applies to checkpoints exactly as it
applies to the assignments.

## Weeks 1 and 12

Neither is graded.

**Week 1** builds your client evidence workbook, which everything after it
writes into. There is nothing yet to build it from, so it is practice — but if
you skip it, week 2 has nowhere to put its numbers.

**Week 12** is assembly for the investment proposal. It is worth doing for the
obvious reason and is not worth a separate mark.

## What this website does and does not do

This is a static site. **It does not collect, store, mark or transmit anything.**
Checkpoints are submitted the way your course convenor tells you at the start of
semester; nothing on these pages is a submission point, and no page here knows
who you are or what you have done.
