Eun-Ji gives the week 11 lecture on what the law actually requires of a household
that reuses greywater or composts its own excreta.

Their point is narrow and repeatable. The rule most people can quote — untreated
greywater goes subsurface, is never stored, and never includes kitchen water — is
New South Wales law. None of this course's five sites is in New South Wales.
Queensland permits surface irrigation and puts kitchen water inside the legal
definition of greywater. The ACT has no greywater-specific regulation at all.

They are not here to tell you what is legal where you live. They are here so that
you stop assuming you already know.
