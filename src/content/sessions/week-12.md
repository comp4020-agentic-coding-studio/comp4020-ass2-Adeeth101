## Before

Bring everything. Quiz 5 runs at the start of this session, on weeks 11 and 12 and
the integration material.

## In the room

**Run the five checks** from the lecture, in order, on your own design. Most people
fail check 2 or check 3 the first time, usually because a load or a line item from
an early week never made it into the summary.

**Then the cross-site comparison.** Form groups of five, one student per preset,
and take one design idea — an 8 m² aquaponic bed, say, or an extra 20 kL of storage
— through all five sites. The [worked comparison](/method/comparison/) shows how
this is set out. What you are looking for is not which site wins, but which
constraint each site's answer is really about.

**Then the uncertainty list.** Write down everything you did not model and
everything you were unsure of, with a range. This is marked. A short list is not a
strong one.

## Afterwards

The capstone is due in the assessment period. What is left is revision, drawing and
writing — not new subsystems.
