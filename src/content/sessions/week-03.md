## Before

Bring week 2's planting plan. Your prunings estimate comes out of it.

## In the room

**Quiz 1 runs first**, twenty minutes, on weeks 1 to 3.

**Then the sizing.** Substrate available, biological efficiency chosen and
justified, fresh mass out, spent mass out. Two outputs, not one — and the second
one is five times heavier than the first.

**Then the argument.** Somebody in the room will want to feed spent substrate to
the larvae, because every diagram online does. Work out together what 1.46 %
bioconversion means for a 100 kg substrate stream, and where the arrow should go
instead.

## Afterwards

Spent substrate mass and its nitrogen carry into week 10's compost balance. Write
it down now; it is the stream most often lost between weeks.
