## What this tutorial is for

Aquaponics is sized from the feed — not from the fish and not from the plants.
That makes the arithmetic short. The rest of the session is the part the model
cannot see: what you are legally allowed to keep.

**Story stage.** Scoping. Quiz 2 runs at the start, on weeks 4 and 5.

## Open these first

- **Your workbook**, week 4's feed balance — it caps how much of the fish is yours.
- **[Week 5's lecture](/lectures/week-05/)** for the FAO feed-rate ratio and the
  stocking limit.
- **Your home's place and jurisdiction** on its [dossier](/clients/).

### If you missed week 4

Use the labelled practice figure and redo it later: **PRACTICE — assume 20 % of

result for any of the five homes.

## The words this week uses

- **Feed-rate ratio** — grams of fish feed per square metre of grow bed per day.
  FAO Technical Paper 589 gives the range a small system is sized from.
- **Stocking density** — kilograms of fish per 1,000 L of tank. The FAO limit for a
  small system is **20 kg per 1,000 L**.
- **FCR** — feed conversion ratio, feed mass per unit of fish mass gained.
- **Fillet yield** — the edible fraction of a whole fish.
- **Counted protein** — the fish protein grown on **your own** feed. Fish grown on
  bought feed are bought fish.

## Do this

**1. Quiz 2 (20 minutes).** On paper, on weeks 4 and 5.

**2. Size from the feed (25 minutes).** Bed area, feed rate, stocking density, FCR,
fillet yield, in that order. Then check your stocking against **20 kg per
1,000 L** before you go any further; a surprising number of first attempts exceed
it by a factor of two.

**3. Write down the operating conditions (10 minutes).** Temperature band, pH,
alkalinity — the conditions a running system has to hold, with units. Put them on
sheet 3 of your workbook as candidate measurement rows. They are what a monitoring
programme would watch, and in week 9 you will read a troubleshooting log of a
system that lost one of them.

**4. Then the legal work, which is the real activity (30 minutes).** Find the
instrument. Not a forum post, not a supplier's page — the Act, the regulation or
the government page that carries the requirement. Canberra students will find that
the model's own preferred species is the one they cannot keep without a licence.
Queensland, South Australian and Northern Territory students will find their
aquaculture definitions turn on sale, which is why their answer is shorter.

Write down **what you found and where**. You will need it in Assignment 2's
compliance section.

**5. Net the protein off (5 minutes).** Counted fish protein is the small number,
after week 4's import share. Carry that one, not the gross.

## The output

**A sized fish and bed pair**, the species named with its approval pathway and the
instrument cited, and the counted protein figure net of imported feed.

## Check your own before you submit

1. Is the bed area derived from the feed rate rather than chosen?
2. Is stocking density under 20 kg per 1,000 L?
3. Does your species row name its temperature band **and** where that band came
   from?
4. Have you cited an actual instrument — an Act, a regulation or a government page
   — rather than a supplier?
5. Is the protein you carried forward net of imported feed?
6. Are the operating conditions on sheet 3, with units?
