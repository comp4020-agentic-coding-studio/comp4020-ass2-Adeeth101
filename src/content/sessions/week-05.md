## Before

Bring week 4's feed balance. Quiz 2 runs at the start, on weeks 4 and 5.

## In the room

**Size from the feed.** Bed area, feed rate, stocking density, FCR, fillet yield.
Check your stocking against 20 kg per 1,000 L before you go further; a surprising
number of first attempts exceed it by a factor of two.

**Then the legal work, which is the real activity.** Find the instrument. Not a
forum post, not a supplier's page — the Act, the regulation or the government page
that carries the requirement. Canberra students will find that the model's own
preferred species is the one they cannot keep without a licence. Queensland, South
Australian and Northern Territory students will find their aquaculture definitions
turn on sale, which is why their answer is shorter.

Write down what you found and where. You will need it in the capstone's compliance
checklist.

## Afterwards

Carry the counted fish protein — the small number, net of imported feed — into
your protein closure. The gross figure is not yours.
