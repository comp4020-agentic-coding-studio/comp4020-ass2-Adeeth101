## Before

Bring your site sheet and a spreadsheet you can edit in the room.

## In the room

**Do one month by hand first.** Pick January. Compute Ra from latitude and day of
year, then ETo from Equation 52, then ETc at Kc 0.95, then effective rain, then
the deficit. It takes about fifteen minutes and it is the only way to find out
whether your spreadsheet is computing what you think it is.

**Then build the twelve rows.** The common error is using total rainfall instead
of effective rainfall, which overstates what the sky delivers by 20–40 % and makes
every later tank look bigger than it is.

**Then the decision.** Multiply your deficit by candidate planted areas until the
annual irrigation figure stops being absurd. Alice Springs students: you will land
somewhere well under your 400 m². That is the correct answer, not a compromise.

## Afterwards

The demand curve is fixed from here. Weeks 7 to 9 size supply against it. If you
later want to plant more, change the curve deliberately and say why — do not let
it drift to match a tank.
