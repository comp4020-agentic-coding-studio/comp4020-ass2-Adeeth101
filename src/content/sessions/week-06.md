## Before

Bring week 2's yield curve. Without it there is nothing to size against.

## In the room

**Draw both curves on one axis.** Harvest by month, consumption flat. The two
areas you care about are the surplus above the line and the deficit below it, and
storage is sized on the smaller.

**Then the losses.** Nobody has a sourced number for these and the course does not
supply one. State yours, say where it came from, and be prepared to defend the
order of magnitude rather than the digit.

**Then the energy.** Adelaide and Canberra students will find their bench sits in
band at ambient and their load is zero. Darwin and Alice Springs students will not,
and the control load they write down here is one of the few things that can push a
design over 5 kWh/day.

We do not make anything. If you want to ferment at home, use a tested recipe and
its own conditions — the lecture explains why this course will not give you a
general rule.

## Afterwards

Bokashi and silage pre-treatment, if you are using them, become an arrow into week
10. Draw it before the break.
