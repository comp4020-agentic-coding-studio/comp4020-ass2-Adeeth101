## What this tutorial is for

Every stream goes somewhere. Today you tabulate all of them, give each a
destination, and find out whether the arithmetic closes — which is the first week
the separate subsystems stop being separate.

**Story stage.** Design. Your home's four-week food-waste audit replaces the 380 kg
convention you used in week 4. This is the week an arrow drawn wrongly in week 3
shows up.

## The words this week uses

- **Dry matter** — mass after the water is taken out. Streams are compared on a dry
  basis because wet masses of different materials are not comparable.
- **Frass** — insect droppings plus shed skins and leftover substrate from the larval
  unit.
- **Residual line** — the gap you write in to make a balance appear to close. It is
  not a loss term; it is the absence of one.
- **Pasteurisation target** — the time-at-temperature a compost process is designed
  to reach to kill pathogens. It belongs to a named standard, and the standard has a
  jurisdiction.

## Do this

**1. Quiz 4 (20 minutes).** On paper, on weeks 9 and 10.

**2. Tabulate every stream (25 minutes).** Food waste split by type, prunings, spent
substrate, frass, sludge. Mass in **dry matter** for each, destination for each:

| stream | wet kg/yr | dry fraction | dry kg/yr | destination | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kitchen food waste, plant | from your audit | | | | findings page |
| meat and dairy | from your audit | | | | findings page |
| spent mushroom substrate | week 3 | | | | your sheet |
| frass | week 4 | | | | your sheet |
| fish sludge | week 5 | | | | your sheet |

**3. Then close it (25 minutes).** In equals out plus losses. **A balance that closes
only because you wrote the gap into a residual line has not closed** — name the loss
and say what process causes it. Respiration, leachate and volatilisation are
processes; "balance" is not.

**4. Then check the temperatures (20 minutes).** Can your bay actually reach and hold
its pasteurisation target in your climate, in the month it is hardest? Canberra
students should look at a July bay honestly. Alice Springs students should look at
where the moisture comes from. Name the standard you are designing against and whose
it is.

**5. Biogas, if any (5 minutes).** Record it. Do not credit it. Only the digestate's
nutrients count toward anything in this course, and the scope page says why.

## The output

**A closed dry-matter balance** in which every organic stream has a named destination
and every loss term is stated as a process.

## Check your own before you submit

1. Is every stream's mass in dry matter, with the dry fraction shown?
2. Does every stream have a destination — including meat and dairy?
3. Is there a residual line anywhere? There should not be.
4. Is each loss term named as a process, with a reason?
5. Does your compost design name its pasteurisation target **and** whose standard it
   is?
6. Does your food-waste input come from your home's audit rather than the 380 kg
   convention?
7. Is biogas, if present, recorded and uncredited?
