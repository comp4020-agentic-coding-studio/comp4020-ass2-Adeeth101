## Before

Bring weeks 3, 4 and 5 — spent substrate, frass and fish sludge. Quiz 4 runs
first, on weeks 9 and 10.

## In the room

**Tabulate every stream.** Food waste split by type, prunings, spent substrate,
frass, sludge. Mass in dry matter for each, destination for each.

**Then close it.** In equals out plus losses. A balance that closes only because
you wrote the gap into a residual line has not closed; name the loss and say what
process causes it.

**Then check the temperatures.** Can your bay actually reach and hold 55 °C in
your climate, in the month it is hardest? Canberra students should look at a July
bay honestly. Alice Springs students should look at where the moisture comes from.
