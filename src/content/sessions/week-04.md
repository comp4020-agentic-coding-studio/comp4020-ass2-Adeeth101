## Before

Bring the food-waste figure from week 1 and your site's thermal factor from the
lecture.

## In the room

**Run the chain.** 380 kg, then the plant-based share, then dry matter, then
bioconversion, then your thermal factor. Five multiplications, and everyone in the
room gets a different answer because of the last one.

**Then the choice that matters.** Rear outdoors and accept your climate's factor,
or rear indoors at about 20 °C and take roughly 0.53 everywhere at the cost of a
counted heating load. Canberra students effectively have no choice. Darwin students
should be able to say why they would decline.

**Then draw the import.** Every diagram in the room gets an arrow coming in from
outside, labelled with a percentage. If yours does not, you have made an
arithmetic error.

## Afterwards

The feed mass balance goes into week 5, where it caps how much of your fish counts
as yours.
