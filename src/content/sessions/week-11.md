## Before

Bring week 2's planted area. Quiz 5 is next week, not this one.

## In the room

**Size the chamber.** Storage band from your site's annual mean, then capacity.
Canberra and Adelaide students will find they need roughly 75 % more volume than
the warm sites for the same four people, which is worth sitting with.

**Then the nitrogen.** Absorbable N against 13.6 kg. Everybody's surplus is large.
The activity is deciding where it goes — extended storage, alkaline treatment,
export to a larger area, or reduced recovery in the first place — and costing that
decision.

**Then the citation exercise**, which is the point of the session. Find your
jurisdiction's greywater instrument. Read what it actually requires. Write two
sentences: what the law says, and where this course is stricter. Four of the five
sites will find the course rule is not their law.

## Afterwards

You now have every subsystem. Week 12 assembles them and finds out which one gives
way.
