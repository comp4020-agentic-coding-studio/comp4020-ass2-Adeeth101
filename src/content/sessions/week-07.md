## Before

page.

## In the room

**Validate before you compute.** Put 750 mm and 200 m² into your sheet and confirm
it returns 116,160 L. Only then point it at your own site.

**Then the twelve rows.** Applying the formula to an annual total instead of month
by month is the error that survives longest, because the answer looks plausible. It
overstates a dry site badly: Alice Springs has months where rainfall is below the
2 mm initial loss and the correct contribution is zero, not negative.

**Then the overlay.** Supply curve against demand curve. Most students see the gap
for the first time here, and it is usually wider than they expected and in a
different month.
