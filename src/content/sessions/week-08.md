## Before

Bring week 7's supply curve. Quiz 3 runs first, on weeks 6 to 8.

## In the room

**Build the train left to right** and put a number on each box. The box you cannot
find a number for is the interesting one — mark it unquantified and say what you
would need to measure.

**Then the field-versus-laboratory exercise.** A BioSand filter rates 2.5 log
mature, 3.71 log run continuously, 1.67 log run intermittently, and about 1.1 log
as a field average. Decide which figure applies to a household that draws water in
bursts, and write down why.

**Then the certification check.** If you have written NSF/ANSI 55 Class A, find an
Australian supplier page that claims it. Most will find WaterMark instead, which is
a different scheme answering a different question.

## Afterwards

The train's energy load goes into the week 12 budget. The UV lamp alone is 0.6
kWh/day, continuous, and it is the load most designs forget is always on.
