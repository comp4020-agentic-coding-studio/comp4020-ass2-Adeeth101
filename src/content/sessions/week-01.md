## Before

Nothing. This is the first session.

## In the room

**First forty minutes.** Claim a site. Five presets, no cap on how many students
take each, and no preset is easier than another — the whole of week 1's lecture
was about why that took four rounds of model revision to arrange.

**Next fifty.** Build the sheet. You pull your own climate data from the Bureau's
station page rather than being handed it, because the capstone will need you to do
this again for a dry year, and because a table you assembled is a table you will
notice errors in.

**Last twenty.** Swap sheets with somebody on a different site and find one number
on theirs that has no source. There is always one.

## Afterwards

Your site sheet goes in your own repository or folder and comes to every
subsequent studio. Custom-site requests close at the end of week 3; the approval
rules are on the [sites page](/sites/).
