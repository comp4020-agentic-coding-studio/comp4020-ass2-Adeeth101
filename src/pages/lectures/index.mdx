Each lecture poses a concrete question, names the method that answers it, and
works one example through with real numbers. Every week from 2 to 11 carries a
**Five sites** section, because the answer is different in five places and the
difference is the point.

Weeks 1 and 12 integrate all five sites. In between, each site leads two weeks —
one where its climate helps, one where it hurts — so no student's preset is
quietly the easy one.

Weeks 1 and 9 carry slide decks. They are the two weeks where the argument is
mostly one number changing shape.
