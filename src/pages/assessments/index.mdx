# Assessment

Seven pieces of work in three kinds, totalling 100 %: five quizzes at 8 % each
(40 % together), the **Food Loop Design** at 20 % in week 7, and the
**Site-Specific Off-Grid Master Plan** at 40 % in the assessment period. There is
no oral defence.

Each quiz is five multiple-choice questions on the previous two or three weeks,
sat on paper at the start of that week's tutorial. Which weeks each one covers is
on the [weekly map](/weeks/).

Both design assessments are marked on the same five criteria — calculations and
justified assumptions, loop integration, site fit, data-driven troubleshooting,
and drawings and communication. Both are checked first against two hard
constraints: **$30,000** of new spend including contingency, and **5 kWh a day** of
process energy with uncertain loads at their upper estimate.

The [reference bands](/method/) place your reported indicators against your own
site's thresholds. They are evidence inside those criteria. **A band is not a
grade**, and nothing on this site converts one into a mark.
