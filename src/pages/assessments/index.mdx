# Assessment

Four records, totalling 100 %.

| What | Weight | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Tutorial completion checkpoints** — ten outputs at 2 % each | 20 % | Friday of each week from 2 to 11 |
| **Five quizzes** at 4 % each | 20 % | Start of the tutorial in weeks 3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 |
| **Assignment 1 — Year-long Measurement Programme** | 20 % | 5 pm Friday of week 7 |
| **Assignment 2 — Household Resilience Investment Proposal** | 40 % | 5 pm Friday 18 June 2027, in the assessment period |

There is no oral defence and no examination.

## The shape of it

Half the marks are for work you do in and around the tutorials, week by week.
That is deliberate. Both assignments are assembled out of those weekly outputs,
so a student who keeps up writes the assignment from what they already have.

**Checkpoints are marked on a good-faith attempt**, not on being right: a legible
attempt at every listed output, with assumptions and units shown. Correctness
earns written feedback instead — which is worth more in week 4 than in week 12.
Attendance alone is not completion, and completion does not require attendance.

The two assignments are marked on criteria published in full on their own pages.
Assignment 1 is a **plan for measuring**, not a design. Assignment 2 is a
**comparison and a recommendation** built from the specialist findings supplied
in week 7 — those findings are the same for every student who took your home, so
Assignment 2 does not depend on how Assignment 1 went.

Assignment 2 is checked first against two hard constraints: **$30,000** of new
spend including contingency, and **5 kWh a day** of process energy with uncertain
loads at their upper estimate. Assignment 1 is costed against a separate
**$1,500** investigation allowance.

## Bands are not grades

The [reference bands](/method/) place your reported indicators against your own
site's thresholds. They are evidence inside the marking criteria. **A band is not
a grade**, and nothing on this site converts one into a mark.

## This site marks nothing

Every page here is static. It collects nothing, stores nothing and transmits
nothing — including the practice questions on the quiz pages, which reveal their
own answers in the page for your use only. Submission happens the way your
convenor tells you in week 1.
